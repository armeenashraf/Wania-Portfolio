A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Learning, creating & discovering

I’ve completed my matric and I’m now continuing my studies at Concordia College. It feels like a brand-new chapter, and I’m taking it one curious step at a time.

Outside of class I love drawing, sketching and painting, and I’m always happy to make a little mess while I figure out colours and shapes. I’m not a professional, just someone who enjoys the process, tries new things and learns something more every day.
